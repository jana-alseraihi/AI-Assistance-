Working hours

The working day starts at 7:00 AM and ends at 3:30 PM, Sunday to Thursday.

The lunch break is from 12:00 to 12:45 in the main cafeteria.

Email and accounts

To activate your email, sign in on any company PC with the temporary password from IT, then set a new password.

If your account is locked, call the IT Service Desk at extension 4357.

Dress code

  • Office areas: business casual.
  • Plant areas: coveralls, safety shoes, helmet, and safety glasses.

Your weekly report

Send a short weekly report to your supervisor every Thursday before 1:00 PM: what you did, what you learned, and any blockers.
